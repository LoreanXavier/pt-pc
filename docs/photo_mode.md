# Photo mode

Photo mode pauses play and lets you reframe the scene with its free camera. Use the arrow keys to move through the
panel, P to save a photograph, H to hide or show the panel, the right mouse button to look around, and Esc to leave.
The panel is not included in the saved image.

You can adjust the camera's field of view or focal length, roll, focus, aperture, exposure and aspect ratio. Focal length
controls both framing and depth-of-field blur. Aspect choices crop the centre of the current view; they do not stretch
the image. Color filters are applied only to the photo view and saved image.

The resolution setting offers **Native** and **4K**. Native saves the framed image at the current render resolution.
4K renders the scene again at a size whose long edge is 3840 pixels, after applying the chosen crop; it does not enlarge
the native screenshot. For example, a 4:3 photo is 3840 × 2880 in 4K mode, while square and landscape crops keep their
chosen shape. A tested 960 × 540 view saved at native square resolution as 540 × 540, and its 4:3 4K capture was
3840 × 2880. Repeated captures of the same view were pixel-identical, and the game returned to its 960 × 540 render
size after each capture.

Photographs are saved under `Pictures/PT Photos`. The image uses the selected crop, resolution and filter, while the
game remains paused during the capture.
